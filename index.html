What to Avoid

Red flags.

"Financial wellness" as a descriptor
Exhausted term, no teeth. Every corporate HR program uses it. It signals nothing specific about your methodology and dilutes the positioning.
Anything that sounds like therapy
Scope creep and liability. PTF is coaching, not clinical. When someone needs professional mental health support, you say so and refer out. Draw the line clearly and early.
The word "journey"
Every coach uses it. It's the personal development equivalent of "synergy." You already have better language — "figuring it out," "the climb," "phase III of VI." Use that.

I'm Vincent, the founder of Present Tense Finance. I'm a former tech worker, a former special needs school bus driver, and an aspiring math teacher — and all three of those chapters converge here. I coach people through the financial chaos that follows life transitions — breakups, job loss, grief, upheaval — using a methodology that starts with feelings and lets the numbers follow.
